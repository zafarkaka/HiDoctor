Zafar Group is more than an investment house. We are a collection of visionary entrepreneurs building the infrastructure for the next generation of global trade.

The 25-Year Journey

Starting in 1999 as a boutique trading firm in Dubai's historic business district, Zafar Group has transformed through three decades of industrial shifts. Our founder, Zafar Siddiqui, envisioned a group that could provide end-to-end value from the farm (Tazifoods) to the digital desk (Fonizo).

Today, we leverage advanced data analytics and IoT integration in our logistics units (Limra Traders) to ensure that the global supply chain remains resilient, transparent, and sustainable.
